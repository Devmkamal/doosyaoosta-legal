Delete your دوس يا أسطى account

This page explains how to delete your دوس يا أسطى (Dos Ya Osta) account and the data attached to it. The game is published by Nourbyte.

The game creates a guest account the first time it connects to our server. If you only ever played offline, no account exists and there is nothing to delete.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Email support@nourbyte.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Include the display name and governorate shown in the game, so we can find your account.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm. Deletion cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your guest account and the device identifier linked to it
  • Your display name and governorate
  • Your runs and leaderboard entries
  • Your in-game wallet, loan and upgrades

What is kept

Nothing linked to your account is kept after deletion.
