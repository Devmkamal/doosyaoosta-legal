Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers دوس يا أسطى (Dos Ya Osta), a microbus driving game for Android published by Nourbyte ("we"). Contact: support@nourbyte.com.

What the game collects

  • A device identifier generated by the game engine for your device, used only to create and recognise your guest account so your wallet, loan and upgrades are kept between sessions.
  • A display name and governorate that you choose, shown on the leaderboards.
  • Gameplay data: the runs you complete (line, stops visited, passengers carried, time, earnings, traffic fines), your in-game wallet, loan balance and upgrades.
  • Voice, only in race rooms and only when you turn your microphone on. Your voice is carried live to the other players in that room by Unity's Vivox voice service. We do not record or store it. The game asks for the microphone permission the first time you tap the mic button, not before.

The game does not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts or photos. It has no in-app purchases. The in-game money and loan are fictional and have no real-world value.

Ads

The game may offer optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus (for example, doubling a run's takings). There are no ads you did not ask for, and none during races. These ads are served by Google AdMob. When ads are on, Google's software in the game may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how you interact with the ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK) the game asks for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How it is used

To run the game: keep your progress, check that submitted runs are valid, and show daily leaderboards by governorate and driver. We do not sell this data. We share it only with service providers who work on our behalf (the host of the game's server, Unity for sign-in and voice chat, Google AdMob for ads as described above), or where the law requires it.

Where it is stored and for how long

On our game server, run by our hosting provider. Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We keep it while your account is in use and delete accounts that have been inactive for 24 months.

Your choices

You can play offline; then nothing is sent, and the garage, loan and leaderboards are unavailable. To have your account and its data deleted, see Delete your account.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy at this address and update the date above.
